# Project Working Agreement

Before changing any course, chapter, slide, narration, activity, quiz, audio
file, or SCORM package, read:

- `docs/chapter-production-playbook.md`
- `docs/nasser-character-system.md`
- `docs/client-compliance-audit.md`

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. The approved PowerPoint is the content source of truth. Do not omit,
   summarize, rename, or invent approved learning content.
2. Nasser uses connected Saudi academic storytelling and explains every visible
   idea instead of reading bullets mechanically.
3. Approve slide text and narration before consuming ElevenLabs credits.
4. Use the centralized audio catalog and generator. Never manually create audio
   paths, timestamps, or manifest entries.
5. Delivery requires a fixed ElevenLabs MP3 and character alignment for every
   narration and interaction key. Browser TTS is fallback only.
6. Narration, dialogue, highlights, reveals, and interaction locks use the same
   audio clock. Do not use independent countdowns.
7. Keep the desktop slide composition on mobile through responsive canvas
   scaling. No slide scrolling, clipping, overlap, or off-canvas controls.
8. Decorative ornaments stay behind content. Nasser and his bubble must not
   cover logos, controls, questions, answers, or essential content.
9. Every chapter ends with a five-question scored quiz followed by a narrated
   closing slide containing the score, summary, and thanks.
10. Run the complete validation, build, browser QA, and SCORM packaging
    checklist in the playbook before committing or delivering.

Do not call work complete while audio, visible dialogue, highlights, animation,
or interactions feel out of sync.
