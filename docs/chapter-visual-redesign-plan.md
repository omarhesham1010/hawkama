# Chapter Visual Redesign Plan

Scope: chapters 1-3 only. The approved introduction slides stay unchanged.

## Chapter 1: Governance Foundations

- Issue: several slides read as dense cards rather than visual storytelling.
- Visual concept: institutional governance scene, board/leadership table,
  policy workflow, and oversight objects.
- Text strategy: keep chapter titles and short labels; narration carries detail.
- Motion: reveal labels around a central institutional illustration, then bring
  related policy/control visuals forward with staggered opacity and motion.
- Nasser: explanation/tablet poses for governance and policy slides.
- QA risk: long labels must not clip; labels stay compact.

## Chapter 2: Compliance And Controls

- Issue: compliance concepts are currently box-heavy and repetitive.
- Visual concept: audit/control dashboard, checklist, magnifier, secure records,
  and PDCA style motion path.
- Text strategy: use short labels such as compliance, controls, monitoring,
  awareness; keep detailed explanation in existing narration.
- Motion: central dashboard with orbiting evidence/control labels, progressive
  highlight from policy to monitoring.
- Nasser: tablet and pointing poses support checklist/control explanation.
- QA risk: avoid dashboard/web-app look; keep official Saudi healthcare palette.

## Chapter 3: Risk Management

- Issue: risk slides need stronger visual metaphors than text boxes.
- Visual concept: risk radar, matrix, shield, warning marker, gauge, and
  mitigation path.
- Text strategy: short labels for risk definition, ISO 31000, register,
  indicators, and governance linkage.
- Motion: radar/matrix object appears first, then controls and monitoring
  visuals emerge on a path.
- Nasser: warning/thinking/tablet poses depending on risk, analysis, register.
- QA risk: keep warning visuals professional and not alarm-heavy.
